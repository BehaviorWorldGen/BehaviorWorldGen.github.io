BehaviorFlow

Straight-Driving Scenario

Controllable cut-in and emergency-avoidance behaviors

Variant 1 — Follow
Front-left vehicle merges into the ego lane and maintains speed; the ego vehicle follows.

Variant 2 — Change Lane
Front-left vehicle merges and maintains speed; the ego vehicle changes to the left lane.

Variant 3 — Emergency Avoidance
Front-left vehicle stops abruptly; the ego vehicle changes lane and drives around it.

Intersection Scenario

Controllable yielding between a left-turning ego vehicle and an oncoming vehicle

Variant 1 — Oncoming Vehicle Yields
The oncoming vehicle yields, allowing the ego vehicle to complete its left turn first.

Variant 2 — Ego Vehicle Yields
The ego vehicle yields while the oncoming vehicle crosses the intersection first.
